# Warehouse & Stock Security Policy

**Policy code:** POL-WHS-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** Warehouse department
**Policy owner:** Warehouse Manager · **Approved by:** Savikro Enterprises Management

## 1. Purpose

Savikro's warehouse holds LS Electric circuit breakers, contactors, relays, drives and control equipment that our customers install in buildings and factories. Stock that goes missing costs the business money. Stock that is **counterfeit or tampered with** can cause fires, injuries and serious damage to our name and to the LS Electric brand.

This policy sets out how warehouse staff keep stock, stock records and warehouse systems secure. It adds to POL-AUP-001, POL-ACP-001 and POL-DHC-001, which also apply.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to everyone in the Warehouse department, including storekeepers, loaders and Savikro drivers. It covers the warehouse building and yard, goods received, goods released, stock records, warehouse terminals and handheld scanners, and returned or damaged goods.

## 3. Warehouse access

1. Only authorised staff may enter storage areas. External doors and gates are kept locked outside loading times.
2. Visitors, contractors and drivers from other companies must sign in, wear a visitor pass and be accompanied at all times.
3. Keys and access cards are personal. Never lend them. Report a lost key or card to the Warehouse Manager the same day.
4. High-value items — such as moulded-case and air circuit breakers, drives and PLCs — are kept in the secured area, which only named staff may open.

## 4. Receiving goods

1. Check every delivery against the purchase order and the supplier's delivery note before accepting it into stock.
2. Inspect packaging, labels, serial numbers and markings. Look for signs of counterfeit or tampered goods: poor print quality, misspellings, mismatched or missing serial numbers, broken seals, or packaging that differs from previous genuine stock.
3. **Do not accept an unexpected delivery into stock.** Hold it in the quarantine area and inform the Warehouse Manager.
4. Suspected counterfeit or tampered goods must be quarantined, labelled "Do not issue", photographed, and reported on the Platform. They must never be sold, used or returned to the shelf until cleared.

## 5. Stock records

1. Record every stock movement in the system under **your own login**, at the time it happens.
2. Stock adjustments and write-offs need the Warehouse Manager's approval, with the reason recorded.
3. Never change a record to hide a shortage or a mistake. Report the difference so that it can be investigated — honest mistakes are not punished; hidden ones are.
4. Stock levels and incoming shipment details are **Confidential** and are shared only as the job requires.

## 6. Releasing goods

1. Release goods only against a valid order or delivery note in the system.
2. Check the identity of anyone collecting goods against the authorised collector on the order **before** releasing anything.
3. A change of collector or delivery address must be confirmed by Sales with the customer, using a phone number in company records, before the goods leave.
4. Delivery notes given to drivers show only the information needed for the delivery — never prices or other customers' details.
5. Obtain a signed proof of delivery for every dispatch.

## 7. Terminals and handheld scanners

1. Use your own account. Lock the terminal (Windows key + L) whenever you step away, and log out at the end of your shift.
2. Never plug unknown USB drives or personal phones into warehouse terminals.
3. Do not install apps or games on terminals or scanners.
4. Return handheld scanners to their charging station at the end of each shift. Report a missing scanner straight away.

## 8. Returned and damaged goods

1. Inspect every return, record it in the system, and keep it separate from saleable stock.
2. Returned goods must not go back on the shelf until checked and approved by the Warehouse Manager.
3. Damaged electrical equipment must not be sold or given away. It is disposed of through the route approved by management.

## 9. Breaches of this policy

Releasing goods without the checks in Section 6, accepting unchecked deliveries, or altering stock records without authority may lead to disciplinary action and, where theft or fraud is involved, to a police report.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year by the Warehouse Manager and the Administrator.
