# Sales Information Security Policy

**Policy code:** POL-SAL-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** Sales department
**Policy owner:** Sales Manager · **Approved by:** Savikro Enterprises Management

## 1. Purpose

Sales staff hold the information competitors most want: what we charge each customer, which projects we are bidding for, and who our contractors and dealers are. This policy sets out how that information is protected so that Savikro keeps its customers' trust and its position as the LS Electric distributor.

It adds Sales-specific rules to the company-wide policies. Where this policy is silent, POL-DHC-001 (Information Classification & Data Handling) and POL-EML-001 (Email Security & Incident Reporting) apply.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to everyone in the Sales department, including sales representatives, showroom and counter staff, and anyone preparing quotations or tenders. It covers:

- quotations, price lists and discount approvals;
- tender and project bid documents, including drawings and specifications received from contractors;
- customer and dealer records, including contact, delivery, billing and credit information;
- phones, laptops and tablets used for sales work, in the office and on site visits.

## 3. Quotations and price lists

1. Every quotation is prepared for **one customer only**. Start each one from a clean template; never edit another customer's quotation to create a new one.
2. Send quotations as PDF, and only to an email address already recorded for that customer. If a customer asks for a quotation to go to a new address, confirm it by phone using the number in company records first.
3. Never reveal cost prices, LS Electric supplier pricing, margins or another customer's discount — in writing, in conversation, or on a screen during a visit.
4. Dealer and contractor price lists are **Confidential**. Mark them as such, and send them only to the dealer or contractor they were prepared for.
5. Discounts beyond your approval limit must be approved by the Sales Manager in writing through company email or systems — not by voice note or personal messaging.

## 4. Tenders and project bids

1. A tender or project bid is **Restricted** until it has been submitted. Only the named bid team may see the pricing.
2. Drawings, panel schedules and specifications received from contractors belong to the customer. Use them only for that project, and do not share them with other customers or suppliers.
3. Never discuss a live bid with another bidder, a competitor, or anyone outside the bid team — including at trade events and supplier meetings.
4. Delete or return project documents when the customer asks, or when the bid is lost and they are no longer needed.

## 5. Customer and dealer records

1. Customer contact details are personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022. Collect only what the sale needs and keep it accurate.
2. Any request to change a customer's **delivery address, billing details or authorised contact** must be confirmed by phone using a number already in company records before it is updated.
3. Do not export customer or dealer lists to personal phones, personal email or USB drives.
4. Credit limits and payment history are Confidential and must not be discussed with anyone other than that customer and the Administration team.

## 6. Working away from the office

1. Company phones, tablets and laptops must be protected with a PIN or password and lock automatically when not in use.
2. Do not connect to public or customer Wi-Fi for company systems. Use your phone's mobile data or hotspot instead.
3. At customer sites, keep your screen turned away from others, and do not leave printed quotations or price lists on vehicle seats, dashboards or site counters.
4. Never leave a laptop or tablet visible in a parked vehicle.
5. A lost or stolen device must be reported on the Platform as **Lost or stolen device** as soon as you notice, and the Administrator phoned. See POL-EML-001.

## 7. Requests that need checking

Stop and verify — using a phone number from company records — before acting on any of the following:

- an urgent order to be delivered to a new or unusual address;
- a request to change who is allowed to collect or approve orders for a customer;
- a request for "the price you gave another contractor";
- someone claiming to be from LS Electric, a bank or a government office asking for sales figures, customer lists or pricing;
- a new customer pressing for a large order on credit without the normal account checks.

If something feels wrong, report it on the Platform. Reporting a suspicion that turns out to be harmless is never a mistake.

## 8. Leaving the department

When you move to another department or leave Savikro, return all customer records, price lists and devices. Taking customer lists or pricing to a new employer is a breach of this policy and of your employment terms.

## 9. Breaches of this policy

Sharing pricing or customer information without authorisation, or bypassing the checks in Section 7, may lead to disciplinary action and, where customers are harmed, to reporting to the relevant authorities.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year by the Sales Manager and the Administrator.
