# Management Security Responsibilities Policy

**Policy code:** POL-MGT-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** Management department
**Policy owner:** Managing Director · **Approved by:** Savikro Enterprises Management

## 1. Purpose

Security at Savikro depends on management. Managers approve payments and access, hold the distributor relationship with LS Electric, decide how incidents are handled, and set the example staff follow. Attackers know this, and often impersonate senior managers to trick staff.

This policy sets out management's security responsibilities. All company-wide policies also apply to managers, without exception.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to everyone in the Management department, including directors and senior managers.

## 3. Leading by example

1. Managers follow every security policy that applies to their staff, and complete their own policy acknowledgements and training on time.
2. Managers never ask staff to bypass a control — for example, to skip a payment check or share a password — even when under time pressure.
3. Staff who stop to verify an instruction from a manager are doing the right thing and must be supported, not criticised.

## 4. Approvals

1. Payments above the limit set by the Board need two management approvals.
2. Approvals are given through company systems or company email — never through personal messaging apps or voice messages alone.
3. Managers must expect to be called back to confirm an urgent or unusual request, and should tell staff that this is welcome.
4. A request that claims to come from a director but arrives by an unusual route must be treated as possible impersonation until confirmed.

## 5. Access decisions

1. Managers confirm the role and department of new staff before an account is created, and tell the Administrator on the day someone changes role or leaves.
2. Access to Restricted information — such as LS Electric pricing, margins and staff data — is approved only where the job requires it.
3. Managers confirm their staff's access is still correct at each six-monthly access review (see POL-ACP-001).

## 6. Supplier and distributor relationships

1. The LS Electric distributor agreement, commercial terms and supplier pricing are **Restricted**.
2. Do not discuss commercial terms, margins or negotiations in public places, at trade events, or with other suppliers or customers.
3. Contractors and consultants who need access to Confidential or Restricted information must sign a confidentiality agreement first.

## 7. Incidents

1. Management decides how serious incidents are handled, including whether customers, LS Electric or the authorities need to be told — for example, where personal data has been exposed under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.
2. Decisions and actions taken on an incident are recorded in the Platform.
3. Incidents are handled without blame. The aim is to learn and to prevent a repeat.

## 8. Overseeing compliance

1. Management reviews the Compliance dashboard at least monthly and follows up with departments where policies or training are overdue.
2. Security policies are reviewed at least once a year, and updated when the business or the threats change.

## 9. Travel and working away from the office

1. Company laptops and phones must be locked when not in use and kept with you while travelling.
2. Avoid public Wi-Fi for company systems; use a mobile hotspot instead.
3. Do not discuss Confidential or Restricted matters where you can be overheard, such as in airports, restaurants or taxis.

## 10. Breaches of this policy

Because managers set the standard for everyone, breaches of this policy by management are treated seriously and reported to the Board.

## 11. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year by the Managing Director and the Administrator.
