# Information Classification & Data Handling Policy

**Policy code:** POL-DHC-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** All staff
**Policy owner:** Administrator · **Approved by:** Savikro Enterprises Management

## 1. Purpose

As sole agent and authorised distributor for LS Electric, Savikro handles information every day that must not reach the wrong people. A leaked price list can cost us deals. A leaked customer contract can cost us a customer. Leaked supplier information can damage our relationship with LS Electric. This policy explains how to tell how sensitive information is, and how to handle it correctly.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff and to all Savikro information in any form: digital files, emails, WhatsApp or other messages, printed documents, handwritten notes and spoken conversations.

## 3. Classification levels

All Savikro information falls into one of four levels. If you are not sure which level applies, treat the information as **Confidential** and ask your manager.

| Level | Meaning | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Public** | Approved for anyone to see. | Published product brochures, public LS Electric datasheets, website content, advertised retail prices. |
| **Internal** | For Savikro staff only. Low harm if leaked, but not for outsiders. | Staff notices, general procedures, delivery schedules, non-sensitive internal emails. |
| **Confidential** | Could cause real business harm if leaked. Share only with people who need it. | Customer quotations, dealer and contractor price lists, customer contact and account details, stock levels, warranty and certification records, invoices. |
| **Restricted** | Could cause serious harm. Limited to named people. | LS Electric supplier pricing and cost prices, margins, distributor agreements, customer contracts, staff personal and salary data, system passwords. |

## 4. Handling rules by level

### 4.1 Sharing

- **Public:** may be shared freely.
- **Internal:** may be shared within Savikro. Do not send outside the company.
- **Confidential:** share only with colleagues who need it for their job, and with the specific customer or supplier the information relates to. Never forward one customer's quotation, pricing or details to another customer.
- **Restricted:** share only with people approved by management. Never send by personal email, personal WhatsApp or SMS. Never discuss in public.

### 4.2 Email and messaging

1. Before sending Confidential or Restricted information, check the recipient's address letter by letter. Auto-complete often picks the wrong contact.
2. Use **BCC** when emailing several customers or dealers at once so that their addresses are not exposed to each other.
3. Only send Confidential or Restricted information to email addresses already recorded in company records, never to a new address given in an unexpected message.
4. Do not use personal messaging accounts to send price lists, contracts or customer data.

### 4.3 Storage

1. Store work files only in approved company locations (company computers, approved shared drives or business systems), not on personal devices, personal cloud accounts or USB drives.
2. Restricted files must be kept in folders that only authorised people can open.
3. Printed Confidential or Restricted documents must be kept in a locked drawer or cabinet when not in use.
4. Keep desks and warehouse counters clear of price lists, contracts and customer details at the end of each day (clear-desk rule).

### 4.4 Printing and paper

1. Collect printouts from the printer immediately.
2. Do not leave quotations or price lists on vehicle seats, dashboards or customer counters.
3. Delivery notes and invoices handed to drivers should contain only the information the customer needs.

### 4.5 Disposal

1. Shred printed Confidential and Restricted documents. Do not put them in general waste or reuse them as scrap paper.
2. Old computers, phones and storage devices must be given to the Administrator for secure wiping before disposal, resale or reuse. Deleting files is not enough.

## 5. Department guidance

**Sales**
- Quotations are prepared for one customer only. Always start a new quotation from a clean template, not by editing another customer's quotation.
- Do not reveal cost prices, supplier pricing or other customers' discounts in conversation or on screen during customer visits.
- If a customer asks for "the price list you sent to another contractor", politely decline.

**Warehouse**
- Stock levels and delivery schedules are Confidential. Do not share them with drivers, visitors or customers beyond what is needed for a delivery.
- Lock the warehouse terminal whenever you step away from it.
- Check the identity of anyone collecting goods against the order before release.

**Administration**
- Customer bank details, invoices and staff records are Confidential or Restricted.
- Any request to **change a customer's or supplier's bank details** must be confirmed by phone using a number from company records, never a number given in the request itself. See POL-EML-001.

**Management**
- Distributor agreements, LS Electric correspondence about commercial terms, margins and staff data are Restricted.
- Approve access to Restricted information on a need-to-know basis and review it when staff change roles or leave.

## 6. Personal data

Customer contact details, dealer records and staff information are personal data. Collect only what the business needs, keep it accurate, use it only for the purpose it was collected for, and protect it according to its classification. Savikro will handle personal data in line with applicable Sri Lankan law, including the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

## 7. Reporting mistakes

If Confidential or Restricted information is sent to the wrong person, lost, or seen by someone who should not have seen it, report it immediately through the Platform (incident type: *Data loss or exposure*). Early reporting often allows the damage to be contained, for example by asking the recipient to delete an email. You will not be punished for reporting an honest mistake promptly.

## 8. Breaches of this policy

Deliberate or careless mishandling of Confidential or Restricted information may result in disciplinary action.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year and whenever Savikro's systems or business arrangements change.
