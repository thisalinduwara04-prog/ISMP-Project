# Finance & Administration Security Policy

**Policy code:** POL-ADM-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** Administration department
**Policy owner:** Administration Manager · **Approved by:** Savikro Enterprises Management

## 1. Purpose

The Administration team moves Savikro's money and holds its most sensitive records: supplier and customer bank details, invoices, payroll and staff files. Fraudsters target this team because one wrong payment can cost more than months of profit.

This policy sets the controls that protect payments and records. It adds to POL-DHC-001 and POL-EML-001, which also apply.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to everyone in the Administration department, including accounts, HR and office administration staff. It covers payments and remittances (including payments to LS Electric), supplier and customer master data, invoices, payroll and staff records, office systems, and the keeping and disposal of records.

## 3. Payments

1. **Two people for every payment.** One person prepares it; a different, authorised person approves it. Nobody approves a payment they prepared.
2. Pay suppliers only to bank details **already held in company records**. Never pay to account details that appear only in an email or on an invoice.
3. Any payment that is urgent, unusual, or requested outside the normal process must be confirmed by phone using a number from company records — even if the request appears to come from a director.
4. Never make a payment on the strength of an email, text or voice message alone.

## 4. Supplier and customer bank details

1. A change to a supplier's or customer's bank details requires:
   - a written request on the company's letterhead;
   - a phone call to the supplier or customer, using a number **already in company records**, to confirm the change;
   - approval of the change by a second authorised person;
   - a record of who checked it, when, and who they spoke to.
2. New suppliers are set up only after their business registration and tax registration details have been checked.
3. Treat a known supplier suddenly sending new bank details, or an invoice that looks slightly different from usual, as suspicious until it has been verified.

## 5. Staff and payroll records

1. Staff personal files and payroll data are **Restricted**. Only named HR and payroll staff may access them.
2. Paper HR files are kept in a locked cabinet; electronic files in folders only authorised people can open.
3. Payslips and salary information are sent only to the employee's own registered address. Never discuss salary details by phone without first confirming the caller's identity.
4. Personal data is handled in line with the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

## 6. Keeping and disposing of records

1. Financial and payroll records are kept for the period required by Sri Lankan tax and company law, as advised by Savikro's accountant.
2. When records are no longer required, paper is shredded and electronic records are deleted through the Administrator. Old computers and storage devices are securely wiped by the Administrator before disposal.
3. Do not keep finance files only on a computer's desktop. Store them in the approved shared location so they are backed up.

## 7. Office security

1. Collect printouts from the printer immediately.
2. Keep cheque books, company stamps and signed blank documents locked away.
3. Visitors to the office are accompanied and must not be left alone with open files or unlocked computers.
4. Lock your screen whenever you step away from your desk.

## 8. Warning signs and reporting

Report on the Platform, and tell your manager, if you see any of the following:

- a supplier or customer asking to change bank details, especially with urgency or secrecy;
- an invoice that duplicates one already paid, or comes from an unfamiliar address;
- pressure to skip the second approval "just this once";
- a request for staff personal data from someone outside HR.

Stopping a payment to check it is always acceptable. Nobody will be blamed for delaying a payment to verify it.

## 9. Breaches of this policy

Bypassing payment approvals, changing bank details without verification, or disclosing staff personal data may lead to disciplinary action and, where fraud is involved, to a police report.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year by the Administration Manager and the Administrator.
